How Ordering Works

No middleman holding your money — you pay each tenant directly.

1
Add to Cart
Browse products from any seller and add what you like. Your cart groups items by tenant automatically.
2
Pick a Bank
Each tenant lists their own CBE, Awash, BOA & Telebirr accounts. Choose one and transfer the total.
3
Confirm Order
Send your payment proof to the seller on WhatsApp — or just download your invoice if you prefer.
4
Get It
The seller confirms and arranges pickup or delivery. The order is logged to them in the background.
